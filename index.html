Who we are

A space built by young people, for young people.

We started in 2019 as a small weekend gathering of students who wanted somewhere to share ideas. Today Youthwave runs a year-round programme of events, workshops, and creative collaborations open to anyone aged 15–25 in our area.

Everything we do is organised by volunteers. No experience is required — just show up, bring your curiosity, and help shape what comes next.

  • Free and low-cost activities for local youth
  • Mentorship from volunteers and local creators
  • Real projects you can put in a portfolio
